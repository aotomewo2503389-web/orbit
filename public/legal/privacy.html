Orbit by SLAWN

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Orbit by SLAWN (“Orbit,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the Orbit website and study app. Orbit is an independent study service and is not operated by or affiliated with Covenant University, the University of Lagos, or Landmark University.

Information we handle

  • Sign-in information: When you choose Google sign-in, Google and our authentication provider, Supabase, process the information needed to authenticate you. Orbit may receive account details such as your name, email address, and profile image where provided.
  • Profile and study progress: The onboarding details you enter (such as your display name, university, programme, level, and exam period), course settings, answers, scores, and mistake history are saved so your practice can continue across devices.
  • Device storage: Orbit keeps a per-account progress cache and your theme preference in your browser’s local storage. Supabase stores your account’s progress in the Orbit database. Authentication sessions may also be stored by the authentication service in your browser.
  • Questions and app operation: The app requests course questions from Supabase. We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information to use the study features.

How information is used

We use this information to sign you in, save and restore your study progress, personalise your course list, operate practice and challenge features, maintain the service, and respond to requests. We do not sell your personal information.

When information is shared

We use service providers to deliver Orbit, including Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication and database services, Google Fonts for typography, and jsDelivr to load the Supabase client library. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

If you choose a duel or challenge feature, information such as your chosen display name, challenge score, or live match status may be visible to the people who join that challenge. If you create a shareable profile link, it contains a snapshot of your display name, challenge wins, losses, draws, win rate, rank, and Plaid Score. Anyone with that link can view the snapshot, and it may remain accessible wherever the link is shared. Do not share it if you do not want those details to be public.

We may also disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal rights. We do not otherwise share account progress with other users unless you choose to share a challenge or profile link.

Retention and your choices

Your cloud progress is associated with your account and is retained while it is needed to provide the service. Signing out does not delete saved progress. You can clear Orbit’s browser storage from your browser settings; doing so may remove the local cache and theme preference but does not by itself delete cloud data. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of account information, contact us using the support contact published with Orbit. A support email or other direct contact should be provided by the operator before this policy is published.

Security

Orbit uses Supabase access controls intended to restrict account progress to its account owner. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please protect access to your Google account and sign out on devices you share.

Children

Orbit is a general study service and is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to Orbit, contact us through the support contact published with the service so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Orbit changes. We will update the effective date on this page when we do. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use, subject to any notice or consent required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Orbit by SLAWN operator using the support contact published with the service. The operator should add a monitored contact email or other direct contact here before launch.
